# Delivery checklist

## Product

- The primary user task is present and completable.
- Navigation, labels, and actions are unambiguous.
- Loading, empty, error, success, and destructive states exist when relevant.
- No invented capability or misleading placeholder is presented as complete.

## Visual

- The selected direction is visible and coherent.
- Generated assets are inspected, purposeful, and integrated.
- Typography, spacing, alignment, color, and hierarchy hold at target viewports.
- No major element failed the reduction audit.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Semantic controls, keyboard access, focus visibility, labels, and contrast are checked.
- Responsive layouts are checked at representative narrow and wide widths.
- Motion clarifies continuity or state and respects reduced-motion preferences when used.

## Engineering

- Follow the existing stack and repository instructions.
- Run the project's lint, build, test, and verification commands that apply.
- Optimize generated assets enough for their delivery context.
- Report checks actually run and anything not checked.
